# Privacy Policy — FIDELVERSE

**Effective date:** 2026-05-22  
**App:** FIDELVERSE: Learn Ethiopian Fidel  
**Package:** `com.fidelverse.app`  
**Developer contact:** abiysetargewkenaw@gmail.com

## Overview

FIDELVERSE is an offline educational app for learning the Ethiopian Fidel
script. We designed it to work without an account, without ads, and without
collecting personal data.

## Data we collect

**We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.**

Specifically, the app:

- does not require an account or sign-in;
- does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or identifiers;
- does not collect location data;
- does not access contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files outside its
  own app storage;
- does not include advertising SDKs;
- does not include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs;
- does not request the Android `INTERNET` permission.

## Data stored on your device

To make the app work, FIDELVERSE stores learning progress locally on your
device. This may include:

- lesson and session progress;
- mastery and review schedule;
- XP, streaks, missions, and achievements;
- app settings such as theme, sound, and haptics.

This data stays on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to third
parties. You can remove it by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by
uninstalling the app.

## Children and families

FIDELVERSE is an educational app. Because it does not collect personal data,
does not show ads, and does not include external communication features, it is
designed to be safe to use in a family context. Parents and guardians should
supervise device use as appropriate.

## Third-party services

The current version of the app does not integrate third-party services that
collect user data. The app bundles open-source fonts under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1; font files are included locally in the app and are not fetched
over the network.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that affect privacy — for example accounts,
analytics, cloud sync, purchases, or network access — we will update this
policy and the Play Console Data safety declaration before releasing that
version.
